I build [Firstage](https://firstage.ai), a place-based home for everyday records. Photos, coordinates, time, and the people you were with pile up on one primitive, and years later they crystallize into something you can hold. One TypeScript monorepo, end to end: Next.js surfaces, NestJS services, a design system of my own, Terraform underneath.

### Editors are the thread

My first product was marketing automation, and the part I cared about was its editor. It's been editors ever since: rich text, web builders, course builders, photobook editors. Lexical for text, Konva for canvas.

> Generating things got cheap. Having been somewhere did not.

Editors are where lived experience takes shape. A walk becomes a page, and one day a book on a shelf.

### How I work

Every rule I keep has a written reason and a violation you could point at. If nobody can tell whether it was broken, it isn't a rule. It's a preference in a costume.

Boundaries get drawn on purpose, one owner per capability. I'd rather maintain two independent things than one clever abstraction I have to keep explaining.

Nothing dies from one bad decision; it slides. So I write down what sliding would look like before it starts.

### Taste

I learn from products that make hard things feel obvious: Figma, Shopify, Medusa, TanStack. I steal from them shamelessly.
